About VegetableTonnes Platform

VegetableTonnes is a B2B agricultural trading infrastructure engineered to eliminate multiple layers of intermediaries in bulk vegetable supply chains. We connect institutional buyers—including food processing plants, hypermarket chains, hotels, and major wholesale distributors—directly with verified regional packhouses and farming clusters.

Operational Pillars

01. Grade Standardization

Every metric ton of Tomatoes and Potatoes listed undergoes physical sorting and electronic quality auditing.

02. Transparent Discovery

Real-time bulk bidding ensures fair market discovery driven strictly by supply volume and buyer demand.

03. Yard Fulfillment

Buyer-side vehicle assignment enables streamlined weighbridge entry and rapid dock loading.

Tax & Legal Compliance

We operate under strict tax compliance standards. All participating buyers must hold valid GSTIN and PAN registration certificates to ensure frictionless commercial invoicing and weighbridge verification.
